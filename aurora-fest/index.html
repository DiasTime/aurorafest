Questions

Anything else, ask at the info desk inside Gate 01.

What does each pass include?

Circuit covers the main floor and every set in the dome. Prism adds a reserved seat in the sound ring and a fast lane at the gate. Founders adds a balcony box, the lounge and the afternoon artist talks.

Can I get a refund?

You can refund a pass in full until November 27, 2026. After that you can transfer it to someone else once.

Why do I tear the stub?

The stub is your check-in. Staff tear it at Gate 01 and you keep the main pass. It shows your zone inside the dome.

Is the venue accessible?

The floor and the sound ring are step-free, and there is a quiet room next to Gate 02.

Is there an age limit?

Yes, the festival is 18+. Bring a photo ID.

Is Aurora Circuit a real festival?

No. This page is a design concept. The lineup is fictional, no passes are sold and no payment is taken.
